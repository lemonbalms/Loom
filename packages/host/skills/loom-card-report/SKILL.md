# Loom card report

Your final message is the card result. Write it in this shape:

1. `Status:` one of `done`, `partial`, or `blocked`.
2. `Summary:` two or three sentences on what you did.
3. `Changes:` the files you changed, one per line, or `none`.
4. `Checks:` the commands you ran to verify the work and their outcome, or `none`.
5. `Open:` anything left undone or uncertain, or `none`.

End the message with this line on its own:

LOOM-REPORT-END

Write nothing after that line.
